Get found

Turn people searching nearby into guests.

Google profile tune-up

2-3 days

Correct hours, real photos, menu link, booking button and WhatsApp on your Google listing. This is the first thing new guests see when they search nearby.

Starter

AI review replies

2-3 days

Every Google and TripAdvisor review gets a reply in your voice within hours. Reviews with 1-3 stars reach you on WhatsApp first, so you decide how to answer.

Starter

Menu-grade food photos

1 week

Phone shots turned into clean, consistent photos for the menu, Google, Instagram and the delivery apps.

Starter

Reels and TikTok machine

2 weeks, then weekly

Staff film 30 seconds in the kitchen or at the bar. AI edits, adds captions and schedules posts on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.

Why here About 974,000 people in T&T use social media, roughly 64% of the population.

Main

Season calendar

1 week

Campaigns written and designed ahead for Carnival, Easter, Divali, parang season at Christmas and Tobago Carnival in October.

Why here Carnival falls in late February or early March, so the busiest weeks are known a year ahead.

Starter

AI search visibility

1 month, then monthly

When someone asks ChatGPT or Gemini for the best curry crab and dumpling in Tobago or a good lime spot in Port of Spain, your restaurant shows up in the answer.

Main

One-page website

1 week

Menu, photos, booking, WhatsApp button and online orders on one fast page that works on any phone.

Starter

Bookings and orders

Take every booking and order, even when the phone is ringing off the hook.

WhatsApp assistant

1-2 weeks

Answers hours, menu, prices and directions day and night, takes bookings and pickup orders, and hands the chat to a person when it should.

Main

AI phone host

2-3 weeks

Picks up when staff are busy, takes bookings, answers the usual questions and sends you a short summary of every call.

Main

Bookings with reminders

3-5 days

Online booking with automatic WhatsApp reminders and optional deposits for large tables. Fewer empty tables from no-shows.

Starter

Your own online ordering

2-3 weeks

A commission-free ordering page with card payment. Keep the delivery apps for reach and move your regulars to ordering direct.

Why here Local delivery apps include foodDROP in Trinidad and Tappogo in Tobago. Local gateways such as WiPay take payments in TTD.

Main

QR order and pay at table

2-4 weeks

Guests scan a code, order and pay from their phone. Tables turn faster on busy nights and nobody waits for the bill.

Main

Menu in other languages

2-3 days

The menu translated automatically for visitors and Spanish-speaking guests, with allergens clearly marked.

Starter

Guests and loyalty

Know your regulars and give them reasons to come back.

Guest list that builds itself

2-3 weeks

Every booking and order adds to one guest file: name, birthday, favourite dishes and how often they come.

Main

Birthday and win-back messages

3 days after the guest list

Birthday offers and a friendly note to guests who have not visited in 60 days, sent on WhatsApp automatically.

Starter

Digital loyalty card

2 weeks

A stamp card that lives in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. No app to download and no paper cards to lose.

Main

After-visit feedback

3 days

A two-question survey after the visit, with a link to review you on Google. Problems reach you the same day instead of a week later.

Starter

Guest notes for servers

1-2 months

The server's phone shows who is at the table: sixth visit, always orders the pelau, nut allergy. Service feels personal without anyone memorising it.

Chef's special

Kitchen and stock

Know what every plate costs and stop buying what you throw away.

Invoice scanning

1 week

Snap a photo of a supplier invoice. AI reads the items and prices and updates your costs.

Starter

Live food cost per dish

2-3 weeks

Every recipe costed to the cent. When a supplier raises a price, you see which dishes lost margin that same day.

Why here The national food import bill hit a record of about TT$7.3 billion, so imported ingredient prices can move fast.

Main

Menu engineering

3-5 days

Your dishes sorted by popularity and profit into Stars, Plowhorses, Puzzles and Dogs: what to promote, reprice, rework or drop.

Starter

Stock count by voice

2 weeks

Walk the storeroom and say the counts out loud. AI fills in the sheet and flags what needs reordering.

Main

Waste log

2 weeks

A quick photo of what goes in the bin and why. After a month you know exactly where the waste comes from.

Main

Demand forecast and prep lists

2-3 months

Predicts covers for each day from your sales history, weather, public holidays and events, then turns that into prep and ordering lists. Needs about six months of sales data.

Why here Carnival week, Divali and Tobago Carnival change demand sharply. A forecast plans for them.

Chef's special

Money and control

See where every dollar went, every morning.

Daily money report

1-2 weeks

Every morning on WhatsApp: yesterday's sales from the till checked against card deposits, cash and delivery-app payouts. Any gap is flagged.

Main

Voids and discounts watch

1-2 weeks

Voids, discounts and comps by staff member and shift. Unusual patterns stand out before they become habits.

Main

Cash-up check

2-3 days

Cash counted at close against cash sales in the system, shift by shift, with the full history in one place.

Starter

Bookkeeping on autopilot

2-3 weeks

Receipts and invoices sorted into expense categories. Monthly profit and loss and VAT figures ready for your accountant.

Main

Owner dashboard

2-3 weeks

One screen on your phone: sales, food cost %, labour %, average bill, best-selling dishes and review score.

Main

Ask your restaurant

1-2 months

A chat that knows your numbers. Ask "How did Carnival Friday compare with last year?" or "Who sells the most dessert?" and get an answer.

Safety and equipment

Protect the stock, the cash and the building, day and night.

Fridge and freezer sensors

1 week

Small temperature sensors that message you the moment a unit starts to warm up. A freezer that fails overnight can cost a week of stock.

Starter

Smart camera alerts

2-4 weeks

AI added to cameras you may already have: alerts for movement after hours, an open back door or activity at the cash drawer.

Main

Energy tracking

2 weeks

Smart meters and plugs show which equipment uses the most power, so you can fix or reschedule the worst offenders.

Main

Maintenance reminders

2-3 days

Grease trap, hood cleaning, AC service and gas checks on a schedule, with reminders and a log of what was done.

Starter

What it takes from you

No new computers and no IT team. Most of this runs on WhatsApp, Google and the tools you already use.

  • Access to your Google Business listing
  • A WhatsApp Business number
  • The menu, and recipes with quantities where you have them
  • Sales exports from the till or POS (an Excel file is enough)
  • About 30 minutes a week to give feedback
